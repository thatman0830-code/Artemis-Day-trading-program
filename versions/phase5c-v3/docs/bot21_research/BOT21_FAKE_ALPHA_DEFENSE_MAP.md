# BOT 2.1 R20 — Fake-alpha defense map

All 52 frozen R18 failure modes are mapped below. R18's mechanism, detection/evidence burden, prevention and assigned stages are preserved as attributed source text, with stage abbreviations expanded. R20 has not performed the proposed checks. This is a future gate/evidence map, not code, tests, an executable specification or authorization.

R18 supplies qualitative likelihood but no separate severity rating. R20 conservatively treats all 52 as material to any claim that exposes that surface. The five highest-priority risk groups are explicitly marked; these priorities describe plausible impact, not measured incidence. Thus every high-severity concern is included without attributing invented numeric severity to R18.

## Gate semantics

| Gate | Required future decision |
|---|---|
| BEFORE_DATASET | Independently authorize source/partitions and causal observation/target definitions before construction; unknown lineage remains quarantined |
| BEFORE_TRAINING | Establish fit-time eligibility, causal dependencies, frozen comparison universe and isolated systems qualification |
| BEFORE_VALIDATION | Establish original forecasts, selection/calibration separation, denominators and dependence-aware claim assessment |
| BEFORE_OOS | Freeze full research process and complete trial/exposure history before separately authorized untouched confirmation |
| BEFORE_SHADOW | Demonstrate non-authoritative observation semantics, effective permissions, full-origin accounting and independent failure containment |
| BEFORE_PAPER | Separately authorize the operational gate with credible execution economics and state/authority isolation; simulation is not realized proof |

A gate being listed does not permit it to run. Unknown or failed evidence closes the corresponding gate or restricts the claim; score improvement never overrides it. The first listed gate below is the earliest assigned preventive review point in R18, not an assertion that later obligations can be skipped. Owners must be independent authorized reviewers of source/temporal, statistical, systems or operational evidence appropriate to the claim; R20 does not appoint or launch them.

## Five highest-priority groups

1. F01: contaminated/unauthorized lineage survives a new dataset name or matching hash. Independent custody/derivation and partition authority are prerequisites.
2. F05/F08: event/bar labels are substituted for receipt availability, or nearest matching consumes future/stale inputs. Both legs and completed intervals need defendable cutoffs.
3. F06/F09: first surviving rows redefine the open, while exact joins exclude difficult periods. Calendar origin, opening-price validity and population coverage are separate obligations.
4. F30/F31/F34: program-wide search, repeated holdout feedback and incomplete multiplicity accounting manufacture certainty. Record every attempted choice and protect untouched confirmation.
5. F39/F40/F41: omitted costs, benign slippage and touch-equals-fill assumptions manufacture economic value. Timing, units, fill feasibility and uncertainty need independent evidence.

## Evidence handling

The R20 synthesis conclusion ledger assigns FOUNDATIONAL_EVIDENCE, RECENT_EMPIRICAL_EVIDENCE, STANDARD_METHODOLOGY, BOT21_PROSPECTIVE_DESIGN_HYPOTHESIS, TECHNICAL_CONSTRAINT or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, with HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNKNOWN confidence. Strong agreement on a prerequisite does not increase confidence that any model earns profit.

Unresolved empirical alternatives remain open. Exact context/horizon, useful joint encoding, regime representation, minimum independent history, uncertainty under shift, actual GPU workload fit and effective isolation are not settled by this matrix. The scientific-source blocker is not resolved by document completion.

# BOT 2.1 R20 — Provisional prototype shortlist

This is a scientific-priority document, not an implementation plan or permission to start Phase R1. R01 means the completed specialist; Phase R1 means later research. Candidate priority does not predict profitability. No selected model, target horizon, threshold, dimensions, optimizer, schema or software installation follows.

## Admission gate

All future candidates require independently approved source/partition authority, a defensible temporal information contract, a frozen chronological evaluation/selection policy and effective BOT 2.0 isolation. No experiment should be recommended for execution before these obligations are met.

## Minimum scientific questions

1. Does temporal context add over credible static/linear/naïve baselines?
2. Does longer or completed multi-resolution context add when effective information and origins are matched?
3. Does eligible cross-market ES/NQ information improve over isolated instruments without synchronization-selection bias?
4. Do return/distribution and risk targets improve per-task generalization individually or jointly?
5. Can calibrated selective prediction produce informative stable coverage–risk behavior across future conditions?

This is a small hypothesis set, not five mandatory experiments or a complete factorial grid. Each additional representation, loss, horizon or architecture consumes the common research-search budget. No numerical budget is invented from literature.

## Representation and target boundaries

Initial comparison candidates: OHLCV control; causal returns, normalized price, range, volume, volatility and calendar/session/contract/quality context; engineered versus normalized raw versus small hybrid representations. VWAP/relative-volume references require valid opening/history coverage. Completed multi-timeframe or ES/NQ additions are independent bounded questions. Later: event bars and trades/quotes/MBP/MBO/order-flow with their own source/reconstruction authority. Not justified: synthetic queues or future-filled historical features.

Initial target questions: return magnitude, direction as a comparator, quantiles or a bounded distributional alternative, and future volatility/risk. Preserve single-task controls when considering a small return-plus-risk multitask model. Later: defined structure/regime labels, MFE/MAE, barriers/time-to-event and policy-conditioned opportunity. No path target from ambiguous bars, no automatic inherited regime taxonomy, and no trade action.

Loss must match the functional: mean/median/robust differences matter; probability scores, quantile loss, CRPS/likelihood and proxy-appropriate volatility scoring answer different questions. No exact objective, weight, horizon or threshold is frozen by this shortlist.

## Conditional extensions

No explicit regime versus causal observable context is the initial regime question. Filtered latent models, state targets and specialist routing are later distinct hypotheses. Feature drift warrants diagnosis; it alone does not justify retraining. Later retraining/recalibration/threshold changes require mature feedback and evaluation of the entire policy against a frozen reference. Ensembles require complementary forward errors and final combined calibration. They do not automatically enter Phase R1.

## Elimination and insufficient evidence

All families stop at invalid source/availability, leakage, undeclared selection or authority inheritance. Stop a family preference when benefit is not reproducible across eligible forward blocks/seeds, depends on fragile context/normalization choices, harms calibration or coverage, fails under shifts, or adds material complexity without defensible incremental value. Future measured memory/latency or realistic cost/fill infeasibility can eliminate the corresponding operational/economic claim. No universal performance threshold is supported. Sparse independent episodes mean insufficient evidence; they do not establish success or impossibility.

## Fake-alpha failure-mode register

**F01 — Source-authority laundering.** Mechanism: repackage exposed/unauthorized sources under a new clean ID. Why deceptive: hashes and names resemble independent provenance. Likelihood: Elevated from R19's audit-derived unresolved boundary; affected material unknown. Detect: custody, derivation, allowed-partition and exposure records without prohibited contents. Prevent: obtain independent source authority before construction; preserve uncertainty/quarantine unresolved derivations. Stages: D, O, S. Basis: R11, R19.

**F02 — Outcome-driven data selection.** Mechanism: retain favorable periods/sessions after observing difficulty or performance. Why deceptive: exclusions are called cleaning. Likelihood: Elevated with unresolved archive/session coverage. Detect: inclusion rules and exclusion counts/reasons, including failed candidates. Prevent: freeze outcome-independent eligibility; restrict claims to the actual selected population. Stages: D, V, O. Basis: R03, R10.

**F03 — Split/archive boundary contamination.** Mechanism: duplicate events, revisions, windows or targets cross nominal partitions. Why deceptive: distinct files and row indices appear independent. Likelihood: Plausible; archives uninspected. Detect: event identity and input/target interval lineage. Prevent: establish ownership before windowing and purge overlapping target information. Shared legitimate past context is not automatically leakage, but dependence must remain visible. Stages: D, T, V. Basis: R07, R10, R11.

**F04 — Cleaned archive hindsight.** Mechanism: corrected trades, backfills and final aggregates replace first-seen data. Why deceptive: cleaner histories improve scores. Likelihood: Unknown without revision lineage. Detect: first-seen/corrected vintages and publication chronology. Prevent: preserve as-of data or explicitly limit claims to finalized-bar hypotheses; never invent receipt times. Stages: D, V, S. Basis: R11, R16; [S04](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/ohlcv).

**F05 — Timestamp/availability substitution.** Mechanism: expose a start-labelled complete bar at its start or exchange event at local availability. Why deceptive: superficial cutoff inequalities pass. Likelihood: Elevated from R16's lineage uncertainty. Detect: interval endpoints and receipt/publication chain; require future-prefix invariance evidence later. Prevent: distinguish event, interval, receipt and decision clocks; narrow claims when unknown. Stages: D, T, V, S. Basis: R11, R16; S04.

**F06 — Missing observations redefine session open.** Mechanism: reset session age/open-reference price at first surviving row. Why deceptive: consistent features encode acquisition delay. **F07 — Calendar/DST/roll oracle.** Mechanism: timeless hours, UTC-day sessions, future-volume rolls or retroactive adjustments invent patterns. **F08 — Future-nearest/stale ES-NQ synchronization.** Mechanism: nearest uses a later input; bounded event skew hides delay/reuse. Why deceptive: apparent cross-market leadership strengthens. **F09 — Exact-join selection/synthetic continuity.** Mechanism: discard unmatched turbulent bars or fill them as ordinary observations. Why deceptive: clean samples score better with smoother risk. Likelihood: Elevated for exact/asynchronous research. Detect: retained/excluded coverage and carried-state flags; distinguish no trade, halt and loss. Prevent: prespecify missingness estimand; do not fabricate volume or treat filled rows as independent events. Stages: D, V, S. Basis: R05, R16.

**F10 — Clock error becomes leadership.** Mechanism: feed delay, offset and bar phase create lag without information advantage. Why deceptive: plausible ES/NQ narratives rationalize it. ## Limits of the review

The register gives reasons claims can fail, not executable tests, a final temporal contract, strategy, thresholds or architectural shortlist. Source/availability legitimacy, target validity, selection-aware inference, economics and operational feasibility remain different evidence obligations. Statistical adjustments cannot cure leakage retrospectively. This does not resolve specialist disagreements or synthesize R20.

Current 2026 evidence leaves foundation contamination active: TSFMAudit is an empirical detector, not universal proof that an unflagged checkpoint is clean. TIME's updated accepted status changes bibliography, not ES/NQ evidence. No paper was reproduced, model loaded, broker contacted or market data requested.

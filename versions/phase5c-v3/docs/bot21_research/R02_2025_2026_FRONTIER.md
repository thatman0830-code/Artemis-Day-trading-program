# R2 — 2025–2026 time-series frontier

**Status:** independent specialist first pass completed (R2); coordinator synthesis pending. Accessed 2026-09-24. Classification below explicitly separates peer-reviewed papers, preprints, and vendor releases.

## Frontier classification

| Topic | State | Evidence and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Forecasting foundation models / zero-shot | PROMISING for broad forecasting; SPECULATIVE for ES/NQ edge | Chronos-2 (Oct 2025) adds multivariate and covariate-informed in-context forecasting; commercial research report and benchmark results are not market validation [S18]. |
| Benchmark integrity / pretraining contamination | ESTABLISHED risk | 2025 analysis documents overlapping pretraining/evaluation datasets, shock memorization, and inconsistent split practice across TSFMs [S19]. 2026 TIME is a fresh-data, task-centric benchmark preprint, not proof of transfer to futures [S20]. |
| Financial-return transfer | SPECULATIVE | A June 2026 arXiv preprint compares TSFMs with supervised baselines on five U.S. equities and finds gains over random walk small/sparse. Equity daily setting, not peer-reviewed ES/NQ intraday evidence [S21]. |
| Calibration as a distinct evaluation axis | PROMISING / newly measured | ICLR 2026 evaluates calibration on five TSFMs and two baselines; authors report TSFMs better calibrated in their settings. This does not establish conditional coverage under futures regime shift [S22]. |
| Self-supervised / masked / contrastive pretraining | PROMISING research program | General time-series methods may use unlabeled histories, but pretraining corpus provenance and time leakage are first-order controls; financial transfer not established [S19]. |
| Mamba/SSM and attention-SSM hybrids | PROMISING systems hypothesis | Linear-time long context and local/global hybrids motivate study; many claimed gains are benchmark-specific and do not imply better performance at short contexts or finance [S16,S23]. |
| Scaling and MoE | SPECULATIVE for this use | Added scale can increase memory, data, contamination, routing and multiple-testing risks; no demonstrated ES/NQ net benefit. |
| Finance-specific candle foundation models | PROMISING as research baselines; SPECULATIVE as trading systems | Kronos is a peer-reviewed candle-series foundation model, while FinCast is a preprint. Their broad market benchmarks do not demonstrate net ES/NQ intraday performance [S54,S55]. |
| Cross-series context | MIXED | A 2026 Chronos follow-up reports that mixing related financial series can reduce accuracy in tested monthly settings. Cross-market inputs therefore require ablation, not an assumption of positive transfer [S52]. |
| Financial fine-tuning | LIMITED transfer evidence | TimesFM fine-tuning results use financial hourly/daily data and mock-trading metrics, not a prospective ES/NQ intraday execution test [S53]. |

### Synthesis

**RECENT_EMPIRICAL_EVIDENCE:** 2025–26 work moves beyond “forecast accuracy only” toward leakage-aware TSFM benchmarks, multivariate/covariate support, and calibration audits. The evidence supports more careful evaluation, not a decision to adopt a foundation model.

**INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE:** No verified, peer-reviewed 2025–26 source found here proves that zero-shot/few-shot TSFMs generate tradable, cost-adjusted edge on intraday ES/NQ. The named 2025–26 financial models and fine-tuning work are useful candidate baselines, not evidence for deployment. General forecasting accuracy, candle reconstruction, daily equities, and mock-trading metrics are not interchangeable with executable futures returns.

**BOT21_PROSPECTIVE_DESIGN_HYPOTHESIS:** If later permitted, foundation models should be comparison candidates only after pretraining-data provenance/contamination audit, identical rolling-origin inputs and costs, and inclusion of naïve and local supervised baselines. Use no external checkpoint output or future-return performance to shape an R0 decision.

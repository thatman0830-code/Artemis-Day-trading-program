## 4. Scientific objective

Provisionally ask whether a defensible, causally available ES/NQ observation history contains reproducible incremental information about future return location/distribution and future volatility/risk, relative to strong simple controls. Determine where forecasts remain calibrated, where information is insufficient, and whether context or shared representations help across future periods.

Direction is a comparator or derived estimand, not the automatic purpose. Structure/regime can be auxiliary hypotheses only with explicit measurement definitions. “Opportunity” must first be a named outcome or later separately authorized policy-value question; it cannot secretly mean buy/sell. Risk estimation is not risk authorization. No inherited 8-step window, 24-feature limit, A2 structure, target thresholds, regimes, horizons or Phase 5C split is adopted. No trading action is selected.

## 6. Temporal foundation

EQUAL TIMESTAMPS DO NOT BY THEMSELVES PROVE CAUSAL AVAILABILITY. A future temporal information contract must establish source authority and schema/version; exchange event/send times; vendor and local receipt/processing times where available; interval start/end and bar publication/completion; correction vintage and earliest usable time. Unknown availability stays unknown rather than being fabricated from an event label.

Keep product/actual contract, exchange trading date, named session/segment, official calendar effective date and timezone-database identity. Separate exchange session open from cash-open reference, first observation, first trade and opening-price availability. Missing opening rows do not reset official session age. Distinguish no trade, closure, halt, feed loss and unknown missingness. Retain partial/open-reference validity.

Holidays, DST, early closes, maintenance, exceptional halts, expiry and settlement have different meanings. UTC date and the user's local timezone cannot supply exchange session identity. Rolls need actual contracts, mapping vintage and a rule whose inputs were available then; future full-day volume and retroactive adjustments are not permissible historical knowledge.

Exact matching requires comparable completed intervals, semantic clocks, session/contract identities and both legs available by the forecast cutoff. It must also account for excluded observations and selected-population bias. One-sided available-as-of matching is a distinct stale-state hypothesis with signed skew, age, reuse, tolerance authority and explicit gaps. Nearest matching is never permission for a future observation; a later cutoff changes the forecast origin and target. Neither method receives an automatic pass. No tolerance is chosen.

No neural experiment is recommended for execution unless this contract is defendable with separately authorized evidence. A finalized-bar hypothesis with explicitly bounded availability uncertainty is weaker than latency-causal replay. The current audit cannot supply the latter. See R11, R16 and defense F04–F10.

## 13. Nonstationarity

FEATURE DRIFT ALONE DOES NOT JUSTIFY RETRAINING. Diagnose data drift P(X), concept drift P(Y|X), prevalence, calibration drift, relative forward-score degradation and data-quality changes separately. Model aging is evidence of changed adequacy, not calendar expiry. A fixed predictor is the necessary reference.

Future evidence for any intervention must include mature outcomes, stable comparator-relative diagnosis, uncertainty and repeated-monitoring accounting, and evaluation of the complete predeclared adaptation policy. Compare retraining, low-dimensional recalibration and threshold adjustment as different mechanisms; none repairs every failure. Track scaler, detector, optimizer, replay and calibration state at each origin. Never rewrite originally issued forecasts after updating. Causal scheduled rolling/expanding comparisons may be studied later; autonomous TTA, self-modifying updates and confidence-as-label schemes are deferred. R13.

## 17. Prospective validation architecture

Use TRAIN for fitting and training-only transforms; inner chronological VALIDATION for architecture/hyperparameters/checkpoints/target alternatives; distinct CALIBRATION for the frozen output mapping; future development evaluation blocks to assess the entire selected process; and FINAL_UNTOUCHED_OOS only after every adaptive/selection rule freezes. Actual boundaries, durations and number of origins remain unselected. Selection and calibration roles must not be silently merged because the old protocol did so.

Conceptually, for sample i record the input information support I_i, forecast origin t_i, target support L_i and label availability m_i. At fit time f require m_i <= f; at forecast t_i require each input's usable time <= t_i. Remove training samples whose outcome information intersects held-out outcome support or whose maturity violates the fit cutoff. Overlapping legitimate past context is not automatically leakage, but its dependence affects inference. Any additional gap/embargo must be derived from actual lookback, target horizon, corrections, state carry and availability supports; no universal “lookback + horizon” row count is declared. In strictly forward training there are no post-test train rows to embargo, though boundary/maturity gaps still matter.

Session and contract boundaries require explicit state/window continuation rules. Record effective history and warmup exclusions. Training-only normalization, imputation, feature selection and fitted state are required. Nested selection is no cure for source contamination.

Predeclare primary estimands/metrics, model universe, bounded search, seeds, repeated-origin replication, stop rules and full trial ledger, including abandoned trials and downstream policy choices. ## 22. Smallest Phase R1 scientific questions

After independent source and temporal admissibility gates, the minimal core questions are:
Q1. Does eligible temporal context improve return/risk forecasts over naïve, linear and static nonlinear controls?
Q2. Does longer or completed multi-resolution context add information once effective history, origins and search budget are matched?
Q3. Does causal ES/NQ context improve over instrument-isolated inputs without selection from synchronization/missingness?
Q4. Do probabilistic return and risk targets, separately and in a small multitask comparison, improve per-task generalization rather than aggregate loss alone?
Q5. Does calibration with selective prediction yield stable, informative coverage–risk behavior across forward conditions?

These are questions, not a build sequence or an implementation plan. Regime, adaptation and ensemble research are conditional extensions only after core evidence identifies a need. No experiment starts.

## 23. Elimination rules

The shortlist and architecture dossiers give family-specific reasons to stop. General validity failures—unapproved source, undefendable availability, leakage, unknown contamination or authority inheritance—stop every family regardless of score. Otherwise stop or defer a family when benefit is not repeatable on eligible forward development evidence, calibration/coverage fails, seeds or small reasonable perturbations dominate conclusions, added complexity has no meaningful incremental benefit, temporal drift breaks its assumptions, or eventual resource/latency/cost feasibility fails.

No numerical performance threshold is justified by these sources. Statistical uncertainty or too few independent episodes means INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, not automatic proof of no effect. Lack of tradability eliminates a trading-use claim without erasing a valid narrower forecasting result.

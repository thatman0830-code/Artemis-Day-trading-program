# Frozen source note: CME performance bonds and historical margins

- Source organization: CME Group / CME Clearing

- Retrieved: 2026-08-27 UTC through public first-party documentation search
- Applicable use: margin semantics and evidence requirements, not numeric ES/NQ margins.

Normalized facts retained: CME describes performance bonds as clearing deposits and distinguishes
initial from maintenance margin. Falling below maintenance causes a call back to initial. CME says
requirements vary with product and volatility and identifies historical margin PDFs from 2003 onward.
No effective-dated ES/NQ numerical history for the repository's archived interval was retained in
this milestone; production eligibility therefore remains blocked.

## Resolution count

Fourteen material issues are recorded. Several are resolved by a causal-information-set distinction (items 2–3) or compatible reporting controls (items 8, 9, 12, 14). At least five remain unresolved as exact protocol values/structures: (1) WF and chronological block geometry, (2) primary estimand/metric hierarchy, (3) A0 probability interface/calibration comparability, (4) abstention q and accepted-vs-forced comparison, (5) session/contract deployment policy and synchronization tolerance. Therefore this matrix does not authorize JSON translation or evaluation.

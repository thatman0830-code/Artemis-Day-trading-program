# Trading Brain

## Mission

Build a systematic trading intelligence system that helps me:

- Understand market regimes
- Identify high-quality opportunities
- Detect cross-market relationships
- Track recurring patterns
- Improve trade selection
- Improve execution
- Learn from historical trades
- Continuously refine my trading process

## Core Philosophy

The objective is not to predict the market.

The objective is to identify:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What confirms the thesis?
4. What invalidates the thesis?
5. Where is the asymmetric opportunity?
6. When should I stay out?

## System Architecture

TradingView → Market Data

Claude → Analysis / Reasoning

Obsidian → Memory / Historical Record

Trading Results → Feedback

Historical Patterns → Improved Decision Making

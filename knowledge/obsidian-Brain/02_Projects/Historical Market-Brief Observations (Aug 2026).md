## Observation 1 — Extreme earnings dispersion while the index stayed relatively calm

- **Historical scope / dates observed:** Aug 3–8, 2026 (recurrent), with corroborating single-name moves in the Aug 10–11 briefs.
- **Source briefs:** [[2026-08-03-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-05-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-07-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-08-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-10-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-11-daily-market-brief]]
- **Evidence strength (per approved matrix):** **Strong** (within window).
- **Directly factual / as-reported:** individual single-stock moves on earnings — e.g., AMZN +15.3% (8/3); SHOP +19.1%, AMD/SpaceX-linked ETFs −15–23% (8/5); HubSpot −22.3%, Datadog −18.0% (8/7); Atlassian +35.3% vs Trade Desk −21.9% same day (8/8) — occurring while index-level day changes stayed small.
- **Historical AI interpretation:** the framing that this is a "stock-picker's/day-trader's tape, not a buy-the-index tape," and that "index calm hides violent single-name risk."
- **Internally later-verified within Aug 3–11:** the phenomenon recurred across at least four consecutive briefs (8/3, 8/5, 8/7, 8/8) — internal recurrence, not external confirmation.
- **Remains unresolved:** whether this holds outside this earnings-heavy, single-environment window; no cross-regime evidence exists in the archive.
- **Why it may be useful:** context for index-vs-single-name risk and for interpreting muted index days as potentially masking dispersion (Master Script §05 breadth/leadership).
- **Independent future confirmation would look like:** repeated earnings seasons, in **different** regimes, showing frequent ±15%+ single-name reactions while index daily ranges stay compressed.
- **What would contradict it:** earnings periods where single-name reactions are muted, or where index and constituents move together (dispersion compresses).

## Observation 2 — Crypto behaving as macro-beta rather than a haven

- **Historical scope / dates observed:** Aug 5–11, 2026 (recurrent), including a live geopolitical risk-off test on Aug 10.
- **Source briefs:** [[2026-08-05-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-07-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-08-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-09-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-10-daily-market-brief]], [[2026-08-11-daily-market-brief]]
- **Evidence strength (per approved matrix):** **Strong** (within window).
- **Directly factual / as-reported:** BTC/ETH/SOL flat-to-down while equities set records (8/5); BTC's pop above $65K coincided with the jobs print (8/8); BTC −1.52% on the geopolitical risk-off day (8/10); BTC/ETH opened lower into pre-CPI caution then recovered (8/11).
- **Historical AI interpretation:** that crypto is trading as "macro beta, not a haven," and that decoupling from the equity rally was a "rotation signal, not a risk-off signal."
- **Internally later-verified within Aug 3–11:** the 8/10 brief records crypto falling on a risk-off geopolitical day (the "failed haven test") — an internal instance consistent with the earlier framing.
- **Remains unresolved:** whether crypto would behave as a haven under a different type of shock; single-environment sample only.
- **Why it may be useful:** candidate cross-asset relationship to monitor (Master Script §05 explicitly lists BTC/ETH confirmation-vs-divergence from equities as a candidate signal).
- **Independent future confirmation would look like:** across multiple future risk-off events in varied regimes, crypto consistently declines with (or ahead of) equities rather than bid as a safe haven.
- **What would contradict it:** crypto rallying as a defensive/haven asset during equity risk-off episodes.

- **Remains unresolved:** whether this is a repeatable behavior at resistance vs. an artifact of this single consolidation; how it resolved past CPI is not in the archive.

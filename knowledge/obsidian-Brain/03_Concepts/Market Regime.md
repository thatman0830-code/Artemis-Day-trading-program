TRENDING
├── Strong breadth
├── Expanding volume
├── Persistent directional movement
└── Pullbacks tend to resolve with trend

RANGE
├── Weak directional follow-through
├── Mean reversion
├── Failed breakouts
└── VWAP / value becomes important

HIGH VOLATILITY
├── Large ranges
├── Fast reversals
├── Wider stops
└── Breakouts can expand rapidly

LOW VOLATILITY
├── Compression
├── Smaller ranges
├── Fakeouts
└── Breakout anticipation becomes important

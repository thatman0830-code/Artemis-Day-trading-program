## What this namespace is

A **reference / confirmation layer** documenting candlestick anatomy, wick interpretation, the six patterns, confluence, and the source's candle-close discipline. Per the source itself, the candlestick pattern is *"Step 3 — the entry trigger, not the entire trading strategy."*

## Authoritative framework

The **Trading Course Method** (`09_Strategies/Trading Course Method/`) **remains the authoritative operational framework.** The candlestick material is subordinate to it and does **not** override market regime, top-down direction, AOI requirements, current market evidence, existing Brain rules, or decision authority.

Candlesticks sit at the **Entry & Confirmation** stage of this sequence:

**Direction → Structure → AOI → Candlestick Confirmation → Candle Close → Entry → Invalidation → Target/R:R**

- Direction, Structure, AOI, Invalidation, and Target/R:R are governed by the Trading Course Method.
- Candlestick confirmation only tells you **WHEN** to enter *once price is at a valid AOI in the top-down direction.*

## A candlestick pattern by itself is not a trade

Per the source: *"A candlestick pattern does not automatically equal a trade,"* and *"No AOI → No entry signal. No confirmation → No trade."* A pattern appearing in the middle of price action (no AOI, against direction) is **not** a setup and must never be traded merely because the shape appears.

## The A / B / C distinction (preserved from the audit)

- **A — Course/Trader Rule:** what the source explicitly teaches (e.g., wait for the candle to **close**; patterns are used *at* AOIs; the six pattern structures).
- **B — Instructor Preference:** presented as a preference/recommendation/tendency, **not** a universal rule (e.g., the Pin Bar "the instructor **prefers** additional confirmation"; "**recommends** focusing on 1-hour charts and above"; "**ideally**…" thresholds).
- **C — Requires Validation:** reliability, effectiveness, predictive value, confluence "strength," performance implications, and pattern edge — **not** established by the source and **not** to be treated as rules (source hedges: "potential," "suggests," "generally," "may").

B and C are never silently moved into A. Where the source does not establish something, the component notes will say **"Not established by the source."**

Engulfing · Pin Bar · Three-Bar Continuation · Three-Bar Reversal · Breakout Candles · Shrinking Candles. *(Structures are A; their meaning/reliability is C. None is a `04_Patterns` entry.)*

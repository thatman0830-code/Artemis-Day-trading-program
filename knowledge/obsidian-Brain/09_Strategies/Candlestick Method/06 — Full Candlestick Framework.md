> [!info] Synthesis / reference map — NOT a standalone strategy or validated edge
> A concise end-to-end map of how the candlestick material fits the existing **Trading Course Method**. Candlesticks are an **Entry & Confirmation layer, not a standalone strategy.** Instructor-derived and **unvalidated**; introduces **no new trading rules.** The Trading Course Method remains the authoritative operational framework. Not a Pattern or Playbook entry. See [[Candlestick Method/_Index — Candlestick Method]].

## Core sequence (integrated model)

```
1. Determine Direction
   ↓
2. Establish Structure
   ↓
3. Identify a valid AOI
   ↓
4. Wait for Price to Reach the AOI
   ↓
5. Look for Candlestick Confirmation
   ↓
6. Wait for the Candle to CLOSE
   ↓
7. Evaluate the Entry
   ↓
8. Define Structural Invalidation
   ↓
9. Target Nearest Significant Structure / Required R:R
   ↓
10. Manage the Trade
```

**"Evaluate the Entry" does not mean an entry is automatically required.** The existing Trading Course Method and user decision authority remain governing throughout.

## The six patterns (short roles only)

- **Engulfing** — Reversal
- **Pin Bar** — Rejection
- **Three-Bar Continuation** — Continuation
- **Three-Bar Reversal** — Reversal
- **Breakout Candles** — Continuation
- **Shrinking Candles** — Reversal

Full definitions are **not** recreated here — see [[Candlestick Method/03 — Six Candlestick Patterns]]. Structures are A; their meaning/reliability is C (unvalidated).

## AOI principle

- **No valid AOI → no candlestick setup.**
- A candlestick pattern **cannot create its own AOI.**
- A pattern appearing in the middle of nowhere is **not** a trade.
- The existing Trading Course Method's AOI framework remains authoritative. See [[Candlestick Method/04 — AOI + Candlestick Confirmation]] and [[Trading Course Method/02 — Areas of Interest]].

## Confirmation principle

- **Confirmation > Anticipation.**
- An unfinished candle is **not** confirmation.
- Per the source, the candle must **close** before the candlestick signal is treated as confirmed. See [[Candlestick Method/05 — Confluence & Confirmation]] and [[Trading Course Method/03 — Entry & Confirmation]].

## Confluence

Multiple forms of analysis **may** agree around the same setup (examples: level + trendline + candlestick pattern; level + trendline + shrinking candles). Confluence is **not** a score, a required number, a probability multiplier, a mandatory gate, or a proven edge — **effectiveness is unvalidated.**

## Candlestick patterns are NOT Brain Patterns

The six instructor-taught candlestick patterns are **reference material.** They are **NOT** validated Brain Patterns and **must NOT** be automatically promoted to `04_Patterns` or `05_Playbook`. Validation would be required before any claim of predictive edge.

## A / B / C discipline

- **A — explicit source definitions/instructions:** candle anatomy; the six pattern structures; the Direction → AOI → candlestick-confirmation hierarchy; candle-close confirmation; the pattern-alone-is-not-enough principle.
- **B — instructor preferences/contextual guidance:** higher-timeframe preference; pin-bar additional-confirmation preference; "ideally" conditions within certain patterns; contextual confluence guidance; pattern-specific stop suggestions where applicable.
- **C — claims requiring validation:** pattern reliability; pattern predictive value; confluence effectiveness; higher-timeframe superiority; wick predictive value; whether candle-close confirmation improves outcomes; any pattern-specific edge; regime dependence. **Do not convert C into rules.**

## Stop / risk management

The source states stops should be placed where the trade idea becomes invalid, and gives pattern-specific stop examples. **Hierarchy:** structural invalidation from the Trading Course Method (`04 — Risk & Targets`) remains **authoritative**; pattern-specific stop examples are **subordinate reference guidance** and must **not** create arbitrarily tight stops. See [[Trading Course Method/04 — Risk & Targets]].

## Target / R:R

The source discusses **2R / 1:2** examples; the existing Trading Course Method already governs target selection (nearest significant structure) and required R:R. **No new R:R rule is created.** See [[Trading Course Method/05 — Full Framework]].

## Not Yet Validated

This material does **NOT** establish: profitability; win rate; expectancy; predictive accuracy; superiority over other entry methods; that more confluence improves outcomes; that higher timeframes are more reliable; that any individual candlestick pattern has an edge; that candlestick confirmation adds information beyond Direction + AOI; or that patterns behave consistently across regimes. These remain **future research/validation questions.** No validation log is created.

## Non-overrides

The candlestick layer **cannot override:** the Master Script; current market evidence; regime rules; cross-asset evidence; hard no-trade conditions; Direction; Structure; AOI requirements; the candle-close requirement; structural invalidation; target/R:R requirements; the Decision Capture Protocol; or user decision authority.

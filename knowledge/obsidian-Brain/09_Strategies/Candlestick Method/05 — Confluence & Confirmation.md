## 1. Confluence (§13)

What the source teaches (wording and uncertainty preserved):

- "A particularly strong setup occurs when **multiple forms of analysis agree**."
- Example: **Resistance + Trendline + Bearish Candlestick Pattern = confluence zone.**
- "The more independent reasons supporting the same trade idea, the stronger the setup **may** become."
- The video's example: **Resistance + descending trendline + shrinking candles = bearish confluence.**
- "The same concept works on the **bullish** side" (the corresponding bullish confluence, e.g., support + trendline + a bullish candlestick pattern, per the source's stated symmetry).

**Not established by the source (do not introduce):** "more confluence = higher probability"; "three confluences are required"; "confluence creates an edge"; a scoring system; a mandatory checklist gate.

**Classification**
- **A:** the source's explicit description/definition — multiple forms of analysis agreeing form a "confluence zone."
- **B:** the instructor's contextual use of multiple agreeing/"independent" reasons.
- **C:** any claim that confluence improves predictive accuracy, expectancy, win rate, or trading performance. **Confluence effectiveness is UNVALIDATED.** (The source only says the setup "**may**" become stronger.)

## 2. Confirmation > Anticipation (§14)

Faithful to the source:

- **Wait for the candle to actually close.**
- Do **not** enter simply because you expect the next candle to confirm your idea.
- An unfinished candle can change dramatically before its timeframe closes ("a candle that looks bullish halfway through its timeframe could finish bearish").
- **"The candle close is the confirmation."**
- The source notes this is especially important for engulfing, reversal, pin-bar, breakout, and shrinking-candle setups.

No additional confirmation requirements beyond what the source establishes are introduced here.

## 3. Confluence does not replace the AOI

The hierarchy is explicit:

**Direction → Structure → AOI → Candlestick Confirmation → Candle Close → Entry Evaluation**

Confluence can be **observed within** that process. It does **NOT** allow:
- a pattern to create an AOI,
- a random pattern to become a setup,
- an entry before candle close,
- a trade when a governing no-trade condition is active,
- a violation of the Trading Course Method.

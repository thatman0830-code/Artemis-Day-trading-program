## Integration principle (read first)

The six patterns are **Entry & Confirmation reference material**. They operate **only** within the authoritative sequence:

**Direction → Structure → AOI → Price reaches AOI → Candlestick confirmation → Candle CLOSE → Entry → Structural invalidation → Target / R:R**

- **A candlestick pattern by itself is NOT a trade.**
- **A pattern outside a valid AOI is NOT a valid setup.**
- **An unfinished candle is NOT confirmation.**
- The existence of a pattern does **not** override current market evidence, the Master Script, regime analysis, risk controls, or decision authority.
- Operational rules are governed by [[Trading Course Method/03 — Entry & Confirmation]] and [[Trading Course Method/05 — Full Framework]].

### Note on pattern-specific stops
Each pattern below carries the source's own stop guidance. **This is pattern-level guidance only.** The **authoritative** rule (Trading Course Method `04 — Risk & Targets`) is that the stop must represent **structural invalidation beyond the AOI**, and must not be arbitrarily tight. Where a pattern-specific stop coincides with structural invalidation, fine; where it would sit **tighter** than the AOI invalidation, **the structural-AOI rule governs.** A pattern-specific stop must not silently override it.

## 1. Engulfing (§5) — Purpose: Reversal

- **Structure (A):** two-candle pattern; the second candle's body **completely covers** the previous candle's body.
- **Bullish Engulfing (A):** first candle bearish, second candle bullish, second completely engulfs the first.
- **Bearish Engulfing (A):** first candle bullish, second candle bearish, second completely engulfs the first.
- **Stated meaning (C — unvalidated):** bullish → "**potential** meaning: bearish momentum **may be** reversing into bullish momentum"; bearish → the inverse. Predictive value not established.
- **Entry/confirmation (A):** bullish → "Buy **after confirmation/close**"; bearish → "Sell **after confirmation/close**."
- **Stop (pattern-specific):** bullish → "Stop **below the relevant wick**"; bearish → "Stop **above the relevant wick**." *(Subordinate to structural-AOI invalidation — see note above.)*

## 2. Pin Bar (§6) — Purpose: Rejection

- **Structure (A):** one-candle pattern — small body, large wick on one side.
- **Bullish Pin Bar (A):** long **lower** wick; "price moved down but was rejected and pushed back upward."
- **Bearish Pin Bar (A):** long **upper** wick; "price moved up but was rejected and pushed back downward."
- **Stated meaning (C — unvalidated):** bullish → "buyers rejected lower prices"; bearish → "sellers rejected higher prices." Predictive value not established.
- **Additional confirmation (B):** the source labels this "Important Rule," but the wording is a **preference** — "The instructor **prefers** additional confirmation rather than immediately entering when the pin bar appears." Example: bullish pin bar → wait for another bullish candle → confirmation → consider long; bearish → wait for another bearish candle → consider short.
- **Stop:** **Not established by the source** (no pin-bar-specific stop stated in §6). Use the structural-AOI invalidation rule.

## 3. Three-Bar Continuation (§7) — Purpose: Continuation

- **Structure (A):** three-candle pattern "designed to identify continuation of the existing trend."
  - **Bullish:** 1) large bullish, 2) smaller bearish, 3) large bullish.
  - **Bearish:** 1) large bearish, 2) smaller bullish, 3) large bearish.
- **Size/relationship (B):** the middle candle "**ideally** shouldn't exceed roughly half the first candle's body."
- **Stated meaning (C — unvalidated):** "temporary pullback followed by continuation of bullish/bearish momentum." Predictive value not established.
- **Entry/confirmation (A):** bullish → "Consider long **after third candle closes**"; bearish → "Consider short **after third candle closes**."
- **Stop (pattern-specific):** bullish → "Stop around the **low/wick of the second candle**"; bearish → "Stop around the **high/wick of the second candle**." *(Subordinate to structural-AOI invalidation.)*

## 4. Three-Bar Reversal (§8) — Purpose: Reversal

- **Structure (A):** three candles "designed to identify a potential trend reversal."
  - **Bullish:** 1) large bearish, 2) smaller bearish, 3) large bullish.
  - **Bearish:** 1) large bullish, 2) smaller bullish, 3) large bearish.
- **Size/relationship (B) + reliability claim (C):** the third candle "**should ideally** have a body equal to or larger than the first candle **for stronger confirmation**." "A larger third candle provides **stronger confirmation**." ("Stronger confirmation" is a reliability claim — **unvalidated**.)
- **Entry/confirmation (A):** bullish → "Enter **after third candle confirmation**"; bearish → same.
- **Stop (pattern-specific):** bullish → "Stop around the **low of the second candle**"; bearish → "Stop around the **high of the second candle**." *(Subordinate to structural-AOI invalidation.)*

## 5. Breakout Candles (§9) — Purpose: Continuation

- **Structure (A):** "occurs when price consolidates and then breaks out strongly" — **at least 3 small-bodied candles** (consolidation) followed by a **large-bodied breakout candle**.
- **Bullish (A):** small candles → large bullish candle.
- **Bearish (A):** small candles → large bearish candle.
- **Detail (A):** "The color of the consolidation candles isn't particularly important. Their **small size** is what matters."
- **Stated meaning (C — unvalidated):** "potential continuation upward" / "potential continuation downward." Predictive value not established.
- **Entry/confirmation (A):** bullish → "Buy at breakout candle **close**"; bearish → "Sell at breakout candle **close**."
- **Stop (pattern-specific):** "Stop around **breakout candle's opening price**" (both directions). *(This can be tighter than the AOI invalidation — the structural-AOI rule governs; see note above.)*

## 6. Shrinking Candles (§10) — Purpose: Reversal

- **Structure (A):** "attempts to identify weakening momentum followed by reversal."
  - **Bullish Shrinking:** 1) bearish, 2) smaller bearish, 3) even smaller bearish, 4) large bullish (three bearish candles progressively shrinking).
  - **Bearish Shrinking:** 1) bullish, 2) smaller bullish, 3) even smaller bullish, 4) large bearish.
- **Stated meaning (C — unvalidated):** bullish → "**suggests**: Selling pressure → weakening → buyers take control"; bearish → "**suggests**: Buying pressure → weakening → sellers take control." Predictive value not established.
- **Additional confirmation (B):** "The video **recommends** looking for the fourth candle to close **beyond an important earlier candle, particularly the second candle**."
- **Stop:** **Not established by the source** (no shrinking-candle-specific stop stated in §10). Use the structural-AOI invalidation rule.

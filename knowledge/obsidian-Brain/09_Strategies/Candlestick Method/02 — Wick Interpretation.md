## Summary

How the source reads wicks: what a long lower wick, a long upper wick, and a small body with similar wicks are said to show. The source frames these as **interpretation** and **evidence of rejection**, not as confirmed signals.

## A — Course/Trader Rules (explicit definitions / descriptions)

- The source states: **"Wicks are extremely important because they show where price traveled but was rejected from."**
- **Long Lower Wick:** "Price moved significantly lower but buyers pushed it back up."
- **Long Upper Wick:** "Price moved significantly higher but sellers pushed it back down."
- **Small Body + Similar Wicks:** "Neither buyers nor sellers clearly dominated."

These are the source's explicit descriptions of what each wick condition *is*.

## B — Contextual / interpretive guidance (the source's stated "Interpretation")

The source attaches an explicit **"Interpretation:"** to each (labeled as interpretation, not as proof):

- **Long Lower Wick →** "Strong buying pressure / rejection of lower prices."
- **Long Upper Wick →** "Strong selling pressure / rejection of higher prices."
- **Small Body + Similar Wicks →** "Indecision / balance."

In its summary rules the source similarly frames long wicks as **"evidence of rejection"** (long lower wick = *evidence of* rejection of lower prices; long upper wick = *evidence of* rejection of higher prices). "Evidence" and "interpretation" are the source's own hedged terms — kept as-is.

## C — Requires Validation

- Whether wick interpretation provides **useful predictive information**, reliability, or trading effectiveness is **not established by the source** and is **unvalidated**.
- "Rejection," "buying/selling pressure," and "indecision/balance" are the source's **interpretations / evidence**, **not** proven predictions of subsequent price movement. Their predictive reliability requires independent validation against my own trading data.

## How this connects (subordinate)

- Wick interpretation is **reference material for the Entry & Confirmation stage** — not a signal on its own. It does **not** override Direction, Structure, AOI, current evidence, or the governing Brain rules.
- It applies only once price is at a **valid AOI** in the top-down direction. **A wick appearing outside a valid AOI does not become a trade.**

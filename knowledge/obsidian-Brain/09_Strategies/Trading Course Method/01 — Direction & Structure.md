## Summary

Top-down analysis to determine **direction** (context, not an entry) and to classify **market structure** as bullish or bearish, including how the valid protected swing is identified and when structure is considered shifted.

## A — Course Rules

- **Top-down analysis:** work from higher to lower timeframes — **Weekly → Daily → 4H → lower timeframes.** Higher timeframes give broad structure; lower timeframes reveal detail.
- **Bullish = higher highs + higher lows (HH/HL). Bearish = lower lows + lower highs (LL/LH).**
- **Snake trick:** trace back from price to the **meaningful directional turn** to find the valid **protected HL/LH**; do **not** label every small swing.
- **Structure protection / structure shift:** bullish structure remains bullish until a **candle body closes below** its protected HL; the inverse applies to bearish structure.
- **Eliminate one side first:** top-down analysis decides whether the setup should be a buy or a sell; then stop looking for the opposite direction.

## Summary

**Stop-loss as structural invalidation**, **take-profit at the nearest structure checkpoint**, and a **minimum 1:2 risk-to-reward.**

## A — Course Rules

- **Stop beyond the AOI:** place the stop **beyond/below the AOI for a buy**, or **beyond/above the AOI for a sell.** If price reaches that invalidation area, the trade idea is **fully wrong.**
- **Stop = invalidation, not arbitrary:** a stop loss is the **invalidation point**, not a random dollar or percentage distance.
- **Timeframe coherence:** use a timeframe coherent with the AOI — a **daily AOI generally calls for daily-context stop and target decisions.**
- **Take-profit at nearest structure:** put take profit at the **nearest structure point** — the next **"checkpoint"** where the market has previously reacted and may react again. This must make **structural sense**, not simply be a preferred multiple.
- **Risk-to-reward:** risk is **entry-to-stop**; reward is **entry-to-target.** The course states a **minimum 1:2** and uses 1:4 to illustrate how a lower win rate can still be profitable.

## B — Instructor Preference

- **4H stop placement (source-labeled preference):** the instructor **notes a preference for 4H stop placement, adjusted to the timeframe of the AOI being traded.** The source presents this as a preference, not a universal rule.
- **"Daily take-profit placement" as a distinct personal preference:** Not established by the source. The source ties **both** stop and target decisions to the AOI's timeframe (daily AOI → daily context) as a Course Rule; only the **4H stop** is labeled a preference.

## C — Requires Validation

- **Performance implications (unvalidated):** the course's R-multiple examples — *five −1R losses and five +2R wins net +5R; seven −1R losses and three +4R wins also net +5R* — the source explicitly states these **"illustrate arithmetic, not expected performance."** Whether ≥1:2 R:R yields profitability in *my* trading is unvalidated.
- **Project Suggestion (source; not a course rule):** record the exact invalidation sentence before entry (e.g., "Long thesis invalid if a closed candle breaks below AOI").
- **Project Suggestion (source; not a course rule):** if nearest structure does not provide at least 1:2 relative to a logically placed stop, label the setup **"No trade — insufficient room"** instead of forcing a farther target.

### Items in the integration mapping that are NOT established by the source
- **"Avoiding unnecessarily tight stops" as a distinct rule:** the source establishes the stop as the **structural invalidation coherent with the AOI timeframe** (plus the 4H-stop preference); a separate "avoid tight stops" rule beyond that is Not established by the source.
- **"Account protection" as a distinct stated rule:** implied via defined-risk invalidation and the minimum R:R; not separately established by the source.

## How this connects

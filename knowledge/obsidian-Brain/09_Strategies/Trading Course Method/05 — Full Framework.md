## The framework (source ordering)

**Direction → Structure → AOI → Entry Signal → Candle-Close Confirmation → Stop / Invalidation → Take Profit / Structure Target → Risk-to-Reward.**

> Master playbook (source): "Top down: identify direction → mark a qualified AOI → wait for price to enter it → wait for a confirmation candle to close → place stop beyond AOI → target nearest structure → require at least 1:2 R:R → execute only if every item agrees."

## A — Course Rules (complete trade workflow, from source)

1. Analyze higher to lower timeframes and classify structure as bullish or bearish.
2. Mark the controlling higher low or lower high using the meaningful turn ("snake trick").
3. Choose only the buy or sell side aligned with that bias.
4. Mark the AOI; note prior reactions and whether the 3-touch rule is satisfied.
5. If multiple AOIs exist, prioritize the clearest area aligned with the higher timeframe.
6. Wait for price to enter the AOI.
7. Wait for a valid confirmation candle to close inside the AOI; do not anticipate.
8. Set stop beyond the AOI, where the thesis is invalidated.
9. Set take profit at the nearest relevant structure point.
10. Confirm the trade offers at least 1:2 risk-to-reward; otherwise pass.
11. Record the plan and outcome for review.

### Pre-trade checklist (from source)
- [ ] Bullish HH/HL or bearish LL/LH identified from top down?
- [ ] Protected HL/LH correctly marked from a meaningful turn?
- [ ] Price at the predefined AOI, with clear reaction history / three touches?
- [ ] Confirmation candle closed inside the AOI?
- [ ] Acting on a closed candle, not an unfinished wick or candle?
- [ ] Stop beyond the AOI, with a known reason it invalidates the idea?
- [ ] Take profit at the nearest structure / checkpoint?
- [ ] Structural target provides at least 1:2 R:R?
- [ ] Entry, stop, target, timeframe, and thesis written before placing the trade?

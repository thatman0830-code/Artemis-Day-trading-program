Nothing in this file promotes anything. Validation is forward-only against my own trading data, and every promotion requires separate approval (Master Script §16–18; Data Contract v1 §4/§5).

## Claims requiring testing (against my own trading data)

- Whether the **snake-trick** identification of the protected HL/LH is reliable and repeatable in my hands.
- Whether **3-touch AOIs** actually produce better outcomes than fewer-touch areas.
- Whether **candle-close confirmation** (vs. anticipating) improves my results.
- Whether a **minimum 1:2 R:R** with nearest-structure targets yields profitability for me.
- Whether the **overall method** constitutes a personal edge. (The source guide is explicitly *"not a promise of results."*)

## Assumptions requiring evidence

- The **"meaningful directional turn"** is a judgment call; my labeling consistency is unproven.
- **"Confirmation, not a pattern name alone"** leaves the qualifying confirmation candles undefined; I need my own explicit criteria.
- **AOI selection** (which area, how many) involves judgment; the source offers a Project Suggestion for ranking but no hard rule.

## Questions raised / discrepancies between the integration mapping and the source

*(These are items the integration mapping listed but that the source does not establish — recorded so they are not mistaken for Course Rules.)*

- **"Nearest AOI first":** source prioritizes the **clearest area aligned with the higher timeframe**, not the nearest AOI. ("Nearest" in the source refers to the **structure target**.) → Not established by the source.
- **"AOI failure" procedure:** source says an AOI is "not a guarantee" and to "keep only the best one or two," but defines no explicit failure process. → Not established by the source.
- **"Do not become attached to an AOI":** not stated by the source. → Not established by the source.
- **"Support/resistance" / "supply/demand" terminology:** source uses **areas where buyers/sellers previously reacted**, not these labels. → Not established by the source.
- **"Avoiding unnecessarily tight stops"** as a distinct rule: source establishes stop = structural invalidation coherent with the AOI timeframe (+ 4H-stop preference); nothing further. → Not established by the source.
- **"Account protection"** as a distinct stated rule: implied via defined-risk invalidation + minimum R:R; not separately established. → Not established by the source.
- **"Daily take-profit placement" as a personal preference:** only the **4H stop** is labeled a preference; target decisions are tied to the AOI timeframe as a Course Rule. → Not established as a distinct preference by the source.

## Preference vs. hard rule (source labeling)

- **Instructor preference:** 4H stop placement, adjusted to the AOI timeframe.
- **Source "Project Suggestions"** (organizational additions, *not* course rules): ranking multiple AOIs; writing an explicit invalidation sentence; the "No trade — insufficient room" label; capturing trade-plan fields in Obsidian; reviewing outcomes as R-multiples.

## Performance disclaimer (from source)

- The R-multiple examples "illustrate **arithmetic, not expected performance**."
- The guide is "**educational … not financial advice or a promise of results**."

## Summary

The **Area of Interest (AOI)** — the specific chart zone, chosen in the top-down direction, where an entry is allowed — including the 3-touch concept and handling of multiple AOIs.

## A — Course Rules

- **Direction first, then AOI:** choose buy or sell, then mark the specific chart area where an entry is allowed. **Do not enter anywhere else.**
- **AOI location within structure:** for a bullish idea, focus on a relevant **lower** area where **buyers previously reacted**; for a bearish idea, a relevant **higher** area where **sellers previously reacted.** An AOI is a **zone, not a guarantee**; it is actionable **only after an entry signal forms inside it.**
- **3-touch rule:** an AOI gains significance when price has reacted from it **three times.** Three touches **do not replace** direction or candle confirmation.
- **Multiple AOIs:** more than one AOI can exist; **retain the higher-timeframe bias** and identify relevant areas in that direction.
- **No valid location → no trade:** entries are allowed only inside the AOI; "trading direction without a location, trigger, stop, and target" is listed as a common mistake.

- **Project Suggestion (source; not a course rule):** rank AOIs by timeframe alignment, clarity of prior reactions, valid touches, and room to the nearest structure target; keep only the best one or two marked.

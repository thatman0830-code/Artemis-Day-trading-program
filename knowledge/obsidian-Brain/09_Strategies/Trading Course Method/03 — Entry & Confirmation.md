## Summary

The **entry signal** — valid only inside the AOI and in the top-down direction — and the requirement to confirm on a **closed candle** rather than anticipate, including how wicks are interpreted.

## A — Course Rules

- **Location + direction gate:** an entry signal matters **only inside the AOI and in the top-down direction.**
- **Confirmation, not a pattern name alone:** the course uses confirmation candlesticks (including **engulfing / doji-style** reactions) but **requires confirmation**, not a pattern name by itself.
- **Candle-close rule:** an unfinished candle can change completely before its timeframe ends. **Wait for the candle to close** before treating it as confirmation or using its **body close** to confirm a structural break.
- **Confirmation over anticipation:** do **not** anticipate an unfinished candle, and do **not** enter before price reaches the AOI. Knowing direction alone is not an entry ("Direction is context, not an entry").
- **Wick interpretation (course-taught):** a wick records where price travelled during a candle but did not remain at close; an **upper wick** can show price pushed higher then retreated; a **lower wick** can show price pushed lower then recovered. Wicks are **evidence of intrabar movement, not final confirmation** by themselves.

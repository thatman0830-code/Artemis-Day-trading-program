# #29.6 — Exact Mechanical Position Lifecycle

## Status
LOCKED_AND_IMPLEMENTABLE

## Ownership
Determines whether a position exists, derives current exposure from actual fills, reconciles working/protective orders and risk, and terminates the lifecycle. It does **not** determine why the position entered or exited. Ownership boundary: #29.1 entry execution/fills; #29.2 permitted quantity/risk; #29.3 protective orders; #29.4 exit resolution; #29.5 costs; **#29.6 position management/lifecycle**; #29.7 accounting.

## Canonical Definition
- A Position is created **only after** the first confirmed entry Fill. `EntryZone touched / EntryOrder created / EntryOrder triggered ≠ Position exists`; `filled quantity > 0 → Position exists`.
- States: `NOT_OPEN → OPEN → CLOSED` (no `CLOSING` state; #29.4 resolves the exit mechanically). A Position object comes into existence only when it becomes OPEN.
- On exit, #29.6 receives `exit_price`, `exit_time`, `exit_reason`, `exit_execution_id` from #29.4 and transitions `OPEN → CLOSED`. On closure `entry_price/entry_time/quantity/stop_price/target_price/exit_price/exit_time/exit_reason` become immutable.
- **OCO after closure:** the winning protective order executed; the remaining OCO order is cancelled.
- **One-position rule:** `OPEN_POSITION_COUNT ≤ 1`. A new entry cannot create a second live position while an existing position is OPEN; the attempt is rejected/ineligible. No pyramiding.

## Position Management and Reconciliation

- `NetQuantity` is the signed sum of immutable entry and exit Fill quantities; requested order quantity never creates exposure.
- `AverageEntryPrice` is the quantity-weighted average of confirmed entry Fills. Filled and remaining quantity are stored separately.
- Every working entry, stop, target, cancellation, replacement, partial fill, and exit remains linked through [[Execution Entity Hierarchy]].
- On the first fill, protective risk must be created/validated immediately for actual exposure under [[#29.3 Protective Orders]]. Missing or conflicting protection produces `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`; exposure remains real and auditable.
- `CurrentRiskDollars` and `MaximumRisk` are recorded; current risk may not exceed the risk authorized by [[#29.2 Position Sizing]].
- Duplicate/conflicting active orders and disagreement between recorded Position quantity and Fill-derived quantity produce `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` without rewriting fills.
- Replacement orders append history and cancel the stale order; they never overwrite it.
- No discretionary scale-in, scale-out, pyramiding, break-even move, trailing stop, or re-entry rule is authorized. Such behavior remains prohibited unless a later owner amendment defines it mechanically.

## Object
```
Position { id; setup_id; trade_id; direction; net_quantity; filled_quantity; remaining_quantity;
  entry_order_ids; entry_fill_ids; average_entry_price; opened_time; working_order_ids;
  stop_order_id; stop_price; target_order_id; target_price; current_risk_dollars;
  maximum_risk_dollars; exit_fill_ids; state; reconciliation_status }
```
#29.6 does not recalculate entry price / quantity / stop / target / slippage / commission / exit price (those belong upstream).

## Hard Invariants
- Position exists only after confirmed fill; single `OPEN → CLOSED` transition
- one live position at a time (no pyramiding)
- closure fields immutable; #29.6 cannot change the exit price
- lifecycle timestamps mirror actual execution chronology (no independent generation)
- position quantity and average price reconcile exactly to fills; partial fills are never rounded up to requested quantity
- scale/trail/break-even behavior is prohibited unless separately defined

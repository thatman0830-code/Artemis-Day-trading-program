# #29.2 — Exact Mechanical Position Sizing & Risk Normalization

## Status
**LOCKED_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED** — logic locked; requires runtime config (RiskPercent, instrument spec). See [[Runtime Configuration Registry]].

## Ownership
Owns position quantity and risk normalization. Consumes the immutable `StopPrice` from [[#13 Stop-Loss Selection]] (never alters it). Produces `ActualRiskDollars` / `MaximumRisk` consumed by [[#29.7.1 Trade Accounting]].

## Canonical Definition
- Risk derives from **pre-fill equity × `RiskPercent`**.
- The upstream stop is **consumed, not altered**; risk distance = `|Entry − Stop|`.
- Sizing is tick / tick-value / contract-multiplier aware.
- Quantity is **floored** to the legal increment.
- Invalid / below-minimum sizing → **rejects** (`POSITION_SIZE_INVALID`).
- Both **actual** and **maximum** risk are recorded.

## Hard Invariants
- `ActualRiskDollars > 0`; non-positive risk is invalid
- quantity floored to legal increment (Amendment 002 R3)
- consumes StopPrice unaltered
- ≥2R price-ratio (owned by #27) is quantity-invariant; consistent with #29.2 dollar risk

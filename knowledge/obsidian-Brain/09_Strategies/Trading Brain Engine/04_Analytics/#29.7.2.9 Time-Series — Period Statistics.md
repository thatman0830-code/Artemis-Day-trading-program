# #29.7.2.9 — Time-Series / Period Statistics

## Status
LOCKED_AND_IMPLEMENTABLE (within [[#29.7.2 Ownership — Scope]]).

## Canonical Definition
Period aggregation using Amendment 002 R1 ([[Time — Period Policy]]): `DAILY / WEEKLY (Mon–Sun ISO) / MONTHLY / FULL_HISTORY`; assignment by `closed_time`; intervals `[start, end)` in AccountTimezone. No-trade period = record with `trade_count=0`, additive totals 0, ratios NULL. Missing period = no record. Provides the period mechanics that [[#29.7.2.15 Strategy-Level Aggregation]] uses to derive [[StrategyPeriodReturnObservation]].

## Hard Invariants
- deterministic period assignment; no-trade ≠ missing; missing ≠ zero

## Dependencies
Upstream: [[#29.7.1 Trade Accounting]]. Consumed by [[#29.7.2.15 Strategy-Level Aggregation]], [[#29.7.2.16 Portfolio-Level Aggregation]].

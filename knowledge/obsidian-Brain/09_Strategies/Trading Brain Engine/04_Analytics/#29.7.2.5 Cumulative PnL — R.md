# #29.7.2.5 — Cumulative P&L / R

## Status
LOCKED_AND_IMPLEMENTABLE (within [[#29.7.2 Ownership — Scope]]).

## Canonical Definition
Trades ordered chronologically by finalized `closed_time` (tie-break `trade_id ASC`). `Cumulative_i = Σ (NetPnL | NetR)_1..i`, starting from 0 before the first trade. Breakeven (`NetR = 0`) leaves cumulative unchanged. This primitive owns the canonical cumulative series; other layers reference it (they do not recompute).

## Hard Invariants
- canonical ordering `closed_time ASC, trade_id ASC`
- cumulative starts at 0; BE unchanged

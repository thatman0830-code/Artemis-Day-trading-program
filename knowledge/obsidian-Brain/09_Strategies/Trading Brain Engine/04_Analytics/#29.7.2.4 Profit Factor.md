## Canonical Definition

Edge cases (locked): `GrossLoss > 0` → finite; `GrossLoss = 0 AND GrossProfit > 0` → ∞; `GrossLoss = 0 AND GrossProfit = 0` → NULL. `ProfitFactorStatus` enum per Amendment 003.

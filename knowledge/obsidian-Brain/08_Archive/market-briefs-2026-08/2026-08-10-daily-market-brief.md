## Plain-English Summary

Two big things happened in the Middle East today: Iran added tough new demands before it will reopen the Strait of Hormuz (a waterway a huge share of the world's oil passes through), and Houthi drones hit a large Saudi oil refinery. Oil jumped almost 5% — its biggest move in months — and energy stocks soared. Silver jumped another 4% and gold climbed toward $4,450 as investors looked for safe places to park money. The stock market itself barely moved, but under the surface traders got more nervous: the "fear index" rose, and Bitcoin slipped back toward a key trend line. Here's why it matters: Wednesday's inflation report was already the week's big test — markets have been celebrating because they believe the Fed can be friendly as long as inflation stays calm. Expensive oil makes inflation harder to keep calm. So the happy story from Friday now has a new complication, and the next two days are about whether oil keeps climbing and whether Wednesday's number cooperates. One bright spot: real-estate stocks, which had been stubbornly weak, finally rallied as interest rates eased — a sign the rate relief is spreading.

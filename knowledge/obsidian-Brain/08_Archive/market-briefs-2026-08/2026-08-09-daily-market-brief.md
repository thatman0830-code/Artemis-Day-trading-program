## Plain-English Summary

It's the weekend, so stocks, gold, and oil are parked where Friday's big rally left them — the S&P at a record, gold and silver coming off their best day in months, all because a surprisingly bad jobs report convinced everyone the Fed won't be raising rates. The only market open, crypto, spent the weekend calmly holding its gains — Bitcoin sat just under $65,000 — and Solana, which had been the weakest of the big three coins, actually climbed back above a key trend line. Markets holding gains quietly is usually a healthy sign. The big event ahead is Wednesday's inflation report (CPI). Here's the simple logic: last week markets celebrated bad jobs news because it means the Fed can be friendly. But that only works if inflation stays tame. If Wednesday's report shows inflation heating up while jobs are shrinking, that's the worst combination — the Fed couldn't cut rates even though the economy needs help — and the celebration could reverse fast. If inflation comes in cool, the rally gets a green light. Also on deck: jobless claims Thursday and retail sales Friday, both of which will show whether the job-market weakness is spreading.

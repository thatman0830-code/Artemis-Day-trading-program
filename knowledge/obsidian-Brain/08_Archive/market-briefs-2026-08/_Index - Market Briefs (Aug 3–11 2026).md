# Archive Index — Daily Market Briefs (Aug 3–11, 2026)

## Purpose

This folder is an **immutable archive** of seven daily market-brief reports produced by a **previous, separate market-analysis workflow**. They are preserved here verbatim as historical source material so the Daily Trading Brain can reference them for context. The reports are stored **unaltered** — no frontmatter, headers, footers, or edits were added to any of them.

**Do not edit, rewrite, rename, or "correct" the archived reports.** If any derived note (observations, concepts, etc.) is created later, it lives elsewhere and cites these files as source — it never modifies them.

## Nature of this material (read before using)

These seven reports are **historical AI analysis**, not verified fact. Each mixes:

- **Historical data as-reported** (index/crypto/commodity prices, rates, catalysts) — accurate only to the extent the previous workflow's sources were, with the reliability caveats noted below; and
- **Historical AI interpretation** (the "Trends Identified" narratives, regime calls, "confirmed/flipped" theses, forecasts, key-level framing).

Interpretation must **not** be promoted into fact merely because it was written confidently. Per the Master Script's Information Hierarchy (§03), this material is **context only** and must never override current live evidence.

**Anti-contamination rule:** the reports terminate on **Aug 11, 2026**, the day before the CPI event several of them build toward. Their forward-looking theses are **not** to be validated or graded using any post–Aug-11 information unless that is explicitly requested in a later, separate step.

## Major source / data limitations (identified during discovery, self-flagged by the reports)

- **WTI crude (CLUSD) & natural gas (NGUSD): plan-gated in every brief** — oil pricing was partially web-sourced throughout.
- **FMP "year high" fields are unreliable** — revised upward across three consecutive briefs; the reports themselves say to trust observed intraday highs instead.
- **Sector-performance figures are equal-weighted** and can be skewed by small-cap outliers (e.g., "Industrials +6.20%" was explicitly flagged as not a cap-weighted reality).
- **Aug 5 sector read was inferred from gainers/losers screens** after the sector-snapshot endpoint dropped — a substitution, not a direct measurement.
- **Treasury-rate data chronically lags** one session in most briefs.
- **Aug 11 brief is entirely web-sourced** (hard FMP rate-limit outage); it self-describes lower precision, and its economics/news calls were auto-denied under an unattended-run permission setting. VIX and SOL are missing that day.
- **No true market-breadth data** anywhere — "N of 11 sectors green" is used as a breadth proxy.
- **Notable-movers lists skew toward micro-cap / leveraged ETFs** and were hand-filtered to recognizable names.
- **Confident conclusions ("regime flipped," "bottom confirmed") are AI interpretation**, in some cases self-graded against metrics the same workflow had set — treat as interpretation, not established fact.

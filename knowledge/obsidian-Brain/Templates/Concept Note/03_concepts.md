# Market Regime - Trending

## Definition (in my own words)
A market environment where price consistently makes directional
progress and pullbacks tend to resolve in the direction of the
prevailing trend.

## Why This Matters
- Momentum and continuation setups may have better conditions.
- Pullbacks can offer opportunities to enter with the prevailing trend.
- Countertrend trades may have lower probability.

## Characteristics
- Strong breadth
- Expanding volume
- Persistent directional movement
- Higher highs / higher lows in an uptrend
- Lower highs / lower lows in a downtrend
- Pullbacks tend to resolve with the trend
